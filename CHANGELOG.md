# Changelog

## 1.0 – 3 November 2026

- First public version, as Stubli Home: Stubli's colours, fonts, wordmark and app icon.
- Demo mode: add ?demo to the address to try it with sample data.
- First-run setup on the tablet: city, calendar, news summaries, wall tips.
- Links and keys are checked when saved, with plain-words help; a news source can be just the outlet's website.
- "Forgot the code?" help on the pairing page, a note on tablets that are too old, and a troubleshooting table.
- Weather from Open-Meteo with "before you go" advice, rain alerts and an animated icon.
- Your day from any iCal calendar, with public holidays for your country.
- News grouped across outlets, ranked by importance, optionally summarised and translated by Google Gemini; headline ticker.
- What's on: your own items, plus Kultur Züri as an optional source for the Zürich area.
- Music on the tablet: YouTube and YouTube Music links play through YouTube's own player, no key needed.
- Optional Sonos and Philips Hue control.
- Settings on the dashboard itself; light, paper and dark looks.
- New dashboards are locked until a device is paired with a code.
