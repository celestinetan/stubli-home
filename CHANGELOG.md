# Changelog

## 1.1 – 4 November 2026

- In Switzerland the weather comes from MeteoSwiss's own forecast (as in its app), not just its model.
- Sonos: album covers show (looked up when Sonos only has them on the speaker), a visual equaliser while music plays, and Recently played instead of the playlists tab.
- Hue colour moods are your Hue scenes under their own names: Galaxy, Fairfax, Soho, Dreamy Dusk and Savannah Sunset, recalled as Hue saved them, each button in the scene's own colour.
- Free museum days and venues more than 100 km from your city are left out.
- Your day shows the next 3 days at a glance, instead of tomorrow in one line.
- Free museum days across Switzerland built in (Zürich, Winterthur, Basel, Luzern, Geneva, Lausanne, Neuchâtel, Lugano), including "first Sunday of the month" rules. Turn them off in Settings → What's on.
- Zürich venues as optional event sources, each with its own switch: Zentralwäscherei (concerts and performances), Kauz, Frieda's Büxe, Hive and Umbo.
- One schedule instead of four, so the Deploy button works on accounts that already use some; news refreshes every half hour to stay well under the free storage limit.

## 1.0 – 3 November 2026

- First public version, as Stubli Home: Stubli's colours, fonts, wordmark and app icon.
- Demo mode: add ?demo to the address to try it with sample data.
- First-run setup on the tablet: city, calendar, news summaries, wall tips.
- Links and keys are checked when saved, with plain-words help; a news source can be just the outlet's website.
- "Forgot the code?" help on the pairing page, a note on tablets that are too old, and a troubleshooting table.
- Weather from Open-Meteo with "before you go" advice, rain alerts and an animated icon.
- Your day from any iCal calendar, with public holidays for your country.
- News grouped across outlets, ranked by importance, optionally summarised and translated by Google Gemini; headline ticker.
- What's on: your own items, plus Kultur Züri as an optional source for the Zürich area.
- Music on the tablet: YouTube and YouTube Music links play through YouTube's own player, no key needed.
- Optional Sonos and Philips Hue control.
- Settings on the dashboard itself; light, paper and dark looks.
- New dashboards are locked until a device is paired with a code.
- One schedule instead of four, so it fits alongside other projects on Cloudflare's free plan.
- An "Update Stubli Home" button (GitHub Actions) brings a copy up to date.
