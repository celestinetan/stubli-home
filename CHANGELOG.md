# Changelog

## 1.1 – 4 November 2026

- Music, two ways: YouTube Music on the tablet is now a step in setup and has its own place on the bar (with the dancers), next to Sonos.
- Easier to fit any city: venues carry their own location, and nearby-only sources (free days, venues, Kultur Züri) appear only within 100 km. "Make it yours" in the README explains how to add your city.
- Your day redesigned as one timeline: a green Now mark, events as blocks in their calendar's colour (the next one larger), free time shown as space, all-day items as pills, and tomorrow below in the same detail (time, end, length, place).
- Full music controls on the bottom bar: shuffle, previous, play, next, mute and volume as − and + in ten steps.
- Type for the wall: Figtree throughout the dashboard (Literata only in the story panel and the Paper look), a sturdier clock and temperature, and darker secondary text.
- Redesign after a UX review, for reading from across the room: a larger type scale, Your day wider with today in one sentence at the top, calmer colours (neutral news cards, a quiet ticker), one line-icon style, and What's on next to Your day in the evening.
- Upright iPads keep What's on, across the bottom. Columns that scroll say "More below".
- Night dims the whole screen; the whole dashboard dims after 10 minutes without a touch; optional clock-only overnight; the layout shifts a few pixels each hour to spare the screen.
- What's on filters in one row; shorter weather tips; a 6-hour temperature and rain curve; headlines never repeat across cards and the ticker; a clearer story panel with Previous and Next.
- One lights control in the bottom bar showing the mood that's on, with a power button; a short note when a mood is switched on.
- The offline notice says when the data on screen was loaded. Settings list everyday choices first.
- In Switzerland the weather comes from MeteoSwiss's own forecast (as in its app), not just its model.
- Sonos: a redesigned music panel without album covers (Sonos often keeps them on the speaker, out of reach), little outline stick figures that groove smoothly on the bar while music plays, with music notes rising behind them (a new crew and new moves each time), a song title that scrolls only when it's too long, a shuffle button, favourites that refresh when the music panel opens, and Recently played instead of the playlists tab.
- Hue colour moods are your Hue scenes under their own names: Galaxy, Fairfax, Soho, Dreamy Dusk and Savannah Sunset, recalled as Hue saved them, each button in the scene's own colour.
- Free museum days and venues more than 100 km from your city are left out.
- The music and lights panels slide up from the bottom and back down.
- Your day shows the next 3 days at a glance, instead of tomorrow in one line.
- Free museum days across Switzerland built in (Zürich, Winterthur, Basel, Luzern, Geneva, Lausanne, Neuchâtel, Lugano), including "first Sunday of the month" rules. Turn them off in Settings → What's on.
- Zürich venues as optional event sources, each with its own switch: Zentralwäscherei (concerts and performances), Kauz, Frieda's Büxe, Hive and Umbo.
- One schedule instead of four, so the Deploy button works on accounts that already use some; news refreshes every half hour to stay well under the free storage limit.

## 1.0 – 3 November 2026

- First public version, as Stubli Home: Stubli's colours, fonts, wordmark and app icon.
- Demo mode: add ?demo to the address to try it with sample data.
- First-run setup on the tablet: city, calendar, news summaries, wall tips.
- Links and keys are checked when saved, with plain-words help; a news source can be just the outlet's website.
- "Forgot the code?" help on the pairing page, a note on tablets that are too old, and a troubleshooting table.
- Weather from Open-Meteo with "before you go" advice, rain alerts and an animated icon.
- Your day from any iCal calendar, with public holidays for your country.
- News grouped across outlets, ranked by importance, optionally summarised and translated by Google Gemini; headline ticker.
- What's on: your own items, plus Kultur Züri as an optional source for the Zürich area.
- Music on the tablet: YouTube and YouTube Music links play through YouTube's own player, no key needed.
- Optional Sonos and Philips Hue control.
- Settings on the dashboard itself; light, paper and dark looks.
- New dashboards are locked until a device is paired with a code.
- One schedule instead of four, so it fits alongside other projects on Cloudflare's free plan.
- An "Update Stubli Home" button (GitHub Actions) brings a copy up to date.
